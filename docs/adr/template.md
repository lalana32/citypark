# ADR-NNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-NNN](NNNN-file-name.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem needs a decision? Which requirements, rules (e.g. R6 in the
[product brief](../product-brief.md)) and constraints apply?

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, followed by the details that matter.

## Alternatives

### Alternative name

Short description. **Rejected because** ...

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier or safer.

### Negative and trade-offs

- What becomes harder, what it costs, what is not covered.

---

Rules for this folder:

- One decision per ADR. Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md` with the next number.
- Every ADR names at least one rejected alternative and at least one concrete trade-off.
- An accepted ADR is not rewritten. If a decision changes, write a new ADR and set the old
  one to *Superseded by ADR-NNN*.
- Add the ADR to the index in [README.md](README.md).
